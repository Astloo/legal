Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how this application ("the App") handles your information when you use it.

1. Information We Access

When you connect your TikTok account, the App uses TikTok's official OAuth authentication. The App may access:

  • Your basic profile information (username, avatar)
  • Permission to upload videos to your TikTok account

The App does not collect your TikTok password and does not access your TikTok messages, contacts, or private content.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information accessed is used solely to:

  • Authenticate your account
  • Upload and schedule videos that you have explicitly created and approved

3. Data Storage

The App is self-hosted. All data, including OAuth tokens and uploaded content, is stored locally on the user's own infrastructure. No data is transmitted to third parties other than TikTok itself for the purposes of publishing content.

4. Data Sharing

The App does not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties.

5. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are stored only for as long as needed to operate the App. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok account settings.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for users under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
